Neyl Gasmi

Graduate student in stochastic modelling (M2MO) at Université Paris Cité and ENSAE. I spent two summers at EuroCTP in Amsterdam, the EU consolidated tape provider, studying price formation in European equities and ETFs across trading venues. I also worked with Alain Celisse (SAMM Lab, Paris 1) on state space models for variable star light curves.

Selected work

State Space Models for Astrophysics

AstroMAMBA, a selective state-space encoder (HiPPO-initialised) trained on 150k irregularly sampled OGLE-IV light curves, learning representations directly from raw observation times without resampling or period estimation. Tested the isometry hypothesis against physical parameter space and benchmarked against Astromer 2, a self-supervised light-curve foundation model. Supervised by A. Celisse.

March to September 2026

E-value Procedures for Online Jump Detection with FDR Guarantees

Online jump detection on high-frequency tick data with a closed-form GROW e-value and online FDR procedures. Benjamini–Hochberg loses FDR control at 5-second sampling, while e-value procedures remain valid under arbitrary dependence. Supervised by A. Celisse.
