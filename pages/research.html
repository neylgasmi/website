Price Formation on the EU Consolidated Tape

EuroCTP, Amsterdam · Jul–Sep 2026 · Internship

Context. MiFID II fragmented European equity trading across dozens of venues, leaving no single reference price. MiFIR Article 22a mandates a consolidated tape for equities and ETFs; in December 2025, ESMA selected EuroCTP as the first equities CTP for the EU.

Mission. Reconstructed a point-in-time European best bid and offer for 109,348 equities and ETFs from the quote and trade feeds of 30+ asynchronous venues. Venue clocks disagree and quotes go stale, so consolidation adds noise correlated with the efficient price: it does not average out, and it biases realised-volatility and spread estimators. Estimated cross-venue lead–lag and Hasbrouck information shares, separating each venue's contribution to price discovery from correlation induced by asynchronous observation.

Data. EuroCTP venue feeds, BMLL Data Lab.

Market Data Quality for the EU Consolidated Tape

EuroCTP, Amsterdam · Jun–Aug 2025 · Internship

Mission. Characterised the market events that distort a consolidated EBBO in BMLL Level 3 data: latency spikes, crossed or locked books, fat-finger prints. Proposed acceptance thresholds conditioned on liquidity regime, since static cutoffs misclassify wide-spread illiquid states as anomalous; author of the resulting methodology note in EuroCTP's ESMA tender response.

Data. BMLL Data Lab.
